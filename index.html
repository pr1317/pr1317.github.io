How I work

Four steps, every engagement

Lean Six Sigma Green Belt. The method is the same whether the output is a dashboard or a retired manual process.

  1. Gather requirements

    Sit with the people who own the process, document the flow as it actually runs, and translate what they need into an analytics deliverable rather than a wish list.

  2. Find the real cause

    Exploratory analysis over the operational data to separate the recurring bottleneck from the symptom people are complaining about. Root cause analysis, not anecdote.

  3. Build the view

    A dashboard that answers the specific question, tracked against SLAs and KPIs leadership already recognises — so it gets used after the first week.

  4. Automate and hand over

    Retire the manual reporting behind it, document the process, and support UAT until the team runs it without me.
